# Temporary visual listings

21 explicitly labelled sample cards: 3 for each of 7 categories. Original SVG illustrations are bundled locally; no external image service, personal contact information or database rows. Samples appear on unfiltered browse pages only, outside real search counts, maps and bookings. Cards open a read-only sample dialog. No provider calls, payment, booking or contact actions.

Remove all samples by setting demoEnabled=false in assets/demo-listings.js, or removing its import/mount from assets/app.js and deleting the demo assets.
