# Card photos and future 360 capture

Cards now expose up to eight ordered photo URLs through the shared listing projection. Multiple images use a native horizontal scroll-snap track, previous/next buttons, keyboard arrows/Home/End, current-photo counter and lazy images. Each photo remains a listing link; horizontal pointer gestures suppress accidental click navigation. Favorites and comparison controls remain separate. Single-image, missing-image and registration plate cards retain their existing presentation. Hover-capable fine pointers get a small lift/zoom; reduced-motion users get no animation. No autoplay or forced touch scrolling lock.

The detail page already supported photo swipes; this release adds card-level browsing. Sellers upload two to eight ordinary photos (JPEG/PNG/WebP, each <=3 MB); the first is the cover. Front-three-quarter, rear-three-quarter, both sides, interior, dashboard and visible defects provide useful ordinary gallery coverage.

360 is NOT implemented by this change. It needs a separate frame-set uploader/viewer and storage/validation limits; the existing eight-photo gallery must not be described as 360. Future capture guidance: stationary vehicle on flat ground with room to walk around, diffuse even light, landscape phone at a consistent height and distance, entire vehicle centered, no ultra-wide perspective changes. Capture an ordered full circle with 24–36 evenly spaced exterior frames (15 or 10 degrees between views), in one direction, starting at the front. Keep zoom/exposure consistent, avoid movement/people, keep doors and wheels unchanged, and do not mix interior or unrelated photos into the sequence. Name frames 001…024 or 001…036 in capture order. A separate exterior sequence is different from a spherical 360 cabin panorama or an interactive 3D model. Preserve originals until dedicated 360 support is released. Uploading these now as ordinary photos will not create a 360 view.
